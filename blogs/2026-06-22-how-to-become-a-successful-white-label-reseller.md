White label reselling is a business model where you buy products or services from a manufacturer and resell them under your own brand name, involving three players: manufacturers, resellers, and end consumers. The post highlights Synthflow as a white label AI voice agent platform with an Agency Plan starting at $30,000, and mentions other white label tools including SharpSpring, SocialPilot, SEMrush, and AppMySite. It advises choosing products based on quality, customization options, scalability, and lower competition.
