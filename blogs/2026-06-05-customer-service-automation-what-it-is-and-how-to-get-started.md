This comprehensive guide explores how businesses can leverage AI and automation technologies to handle repetitive customer support tasks at scale. The article explains that the goal is not replacing human agents but rather freeing them from high-volume, routine queries so they can focus on complex, valuable interactions. It covers four main automation types including self-service portals, AI agents, AI-powered IVR systems, and automated ticketing, while providing a practical seven-step implementation roadmap.
